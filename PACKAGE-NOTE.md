# PACKAGE NOTE — Fiverr-ready (P4: E-commerce Price Tracker)

**Gig title:** I will track your competitors' prices and give you a comparison sheet that updates every run

**One-line pitch:** Stop checking competitor prices by hand. I scrape
the catalogue, store every price with its date, and hand you a sheet
where the next run shows exactly what changed.

## What's included in every tier

- Comparison sheet sorted by category and price, with product links
- Summary with live formulas (cheapest, average, most expensive)
- The price-history database, so future runs show changes, not just lists
- A short report: price range, cheapest/most expensive, what stands out
- Sample shown here (1,000 books, 50 categories, snapshot 31 Oct 2026)
  is the exact template a real order starts from

## What the buyer sends

The competitor pages or sites to track, and which products/categories
matter. Sites that forbid scraping or need a login are declined —
the service only tracks public pages the buyer has a right to monitor.

## Delivery promise

Basic in 2 days, Standard in 3, Premium in 5 — from the day the
competitor list is received. Premium re-runs are scheduled, not
one-off, so the buyer keeps getting the change report.
